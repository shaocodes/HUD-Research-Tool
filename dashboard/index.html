How each measure is defined
  • Gameplay events (full-map opens, navigation errors, hesitations, missed prompts, unintended actions, menu breaks): tagged in the coding app while watching each recording, divided by the session's active minutes. Active time is 30 minutes minus the segments coders marked as static, no gameplay or corrupted.
  • Hesitation: no input for three or more seconds outside a cutscene.
  • Time to objective: seconds from an objective appearing to it being completed, averaged over the objectives completed in that session.
  • SA accuracy: share of post-session situation awareness questions answered correctly, checked against the end of the recording; also split by Endsley's three levels.
  • Gaze on game world: share of cleaned gaze samples outside every HUD area of interest.
